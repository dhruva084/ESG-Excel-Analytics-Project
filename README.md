# ESG Excel Analytics Project

# Project Overview

An Excel-based ESG data analysis project using a fictional dataset of 100 companies. The project demonstrates the use of Excel formulas, data analysis, PivotTables, interactive dashboard features, ESG scoring, risk flagging, and VBA automation.

The project was developed to practice applying Excel to sustainability and ESG-related data analysis.

# Project Objectives

* Organize environmental, social, governance, and financial data.
* Apply Excel formulas to retrieve, analyze, and summarize ESG information.
* Develop composite ESG scores using multiple ESG indicators.
* Categorize companies into ESG risk tiers.
* Analyze ESG performance across industries and countries.
* Create an interactive dashboard for portfolio-level ESG analysis.
* Practice VBA automation within an Excel-based reporting workflow.

# Data Categories

The dataset includes:

* Environmental: Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3 emissions, energy use, renewable energy, water use, and waste.
* Social: Women in leadership, female employees, training hours, employee turnover, and safety incidents.
* Governance: Board independence, ESG committees, ethics violations, and ESG scores.
* Financial: Revenue, operating profit, net income, assets, liabilities, industry, and country.

# ESG Analysis

A composite ESG score is developed using normalized ESG indicators, including:

* Renewable energy percentage
* Women in leadership
* ESG score

The workbook also uses ESG scores to classify companies into 'Low Risk, Medium Risk, and High Risk' categories based on defined thresholds.

# Workbook Structure

The Excel workbook contains the following sheets:

* Company_Master - Consolidated company information and formula-based ESG analysis, including lookups, ESG risk tiers, and selected emissions metrics.
* Environmental - Environmental performance data including Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3, energy, renewable energy, water, and waste.
* Social - Social performance indicators including workforce composition, training, turnover, and safety.
* Governance - Governance indicators, ESG scoring, rankings, normalization, and composite ESG calculations.
* Financial - Financial and company-level information used for analysis.
* Pivot_Tab_Prac - PivotTable-based analysis of ESG scores by industry and country.
* Master_Dashboard - Dashboard-oriented analysis combining ESG, environmental, social, governance, and financial information.
* Risk-flagging - Formula-based identification of company ESG risk tiers.

# Notes & Limitations

The company data used in this project is fictional and was created for learning and portfolio purposes. The project demonstrates Excel-based ESG data analysis techniques and does not represent the actual ESG performance of real companies.

The ESG scoring and risk classifications are based on the assumptions and thresholds defined within the workbook and should not be interpreted as an external ESG rating methodology.
